id: K06
title: Carbon support corrosion
topic: durability
evidence: textbook
tags: carbon corrosion, start-stop, support, Pt detachment
---
High-surface-area carbon oxidises at high potential. During start-up and shut-down, an air/hydrogen front on the anode can push local cathode potentials well above normal operating values, which accelerates carbon corrosion. Consequences are Pt particles detaching or clustering, loss of pore structure and a thinner, more flooded catalyst layer. Mitigations include controlled start-stop purge procedures, graphitised or otherwise corrosion-resistant supports, and keeping the upper potential limit low during conditioning cycles.
