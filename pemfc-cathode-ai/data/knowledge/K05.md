id: K05
title: Platinum-group-metal-free (Fe-N-C) cathodes
topic: catalyst
evidence: literature
tags: Fe-N-C, PGM-free, cost, durability, peroxide
---
Fe-N-C catalysts replace Pt with atomically dispersed Fe-Nx sites in carbon. They are low-cost, but the volumetric density of active sites is lower than Pt/C, so cathodes must be much thicker. Thick layers make water and oxygen transport harder, which shows up as an early mass-transport knee. Durability in acid is the main concern: hydrogen peroxide formed on the sites can react with iron to make radicals that attack the carbon and the ionomer. Present data cannot rank Fe-N-C against Pt/C because ECSIM contains only Pt/C electrodes.
