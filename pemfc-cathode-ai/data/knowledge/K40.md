A catalyst that is active but dissolves is not useful. Standard reduction potentials of the metals give a first screen: Ni, Co and Fe are far below the 0.9 V of a typical cathode, so they are not thermodynamically stable in acid when exposed, whereas Pt is much closer to its dissolution potential and noble elements such as Au and Ir sit above it. Bulk potentials are only a heuristic because nanoparticle size, adsorbates, potential cycling and alloy surfaces shift dissolution. Activity-only screening can therefore recommend candidates that fail in operation; DFT segregation, surface and dissolution energies refine the screen.
