title: Functional choice and the uncertainty of absolute DFT energies

Different exchange-correlation functionals shift adsorption energies of oxygen species by amounts that matter on a volcano whose useful width is a few tenths of an electronvolt. For that reason trends and differences between candidates are more reliable than absolute numbers, and a calculation of Pt(111) with the same settings should accompany every new alloy as an internal reference. Report the functional, the code, the cutoff, the k-point mesh and the slab model with every value.
