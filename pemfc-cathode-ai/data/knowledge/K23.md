title: Measuring ECSA by hydrogen underpotential deposition
topic: metrics
evidence: textbook
tags: ECSA, cyclic voltammetry, HUPD, 210 uC/cm2
---
ECSA of Pt is often estimated from the charge under the hydrogen adsorption peaks of a cyclic voltammogram, using a conventional 210 microcoulombs per square centimetre of Pt. Measurements are done with humidified hydrogen on the counter electrode and nitrogen on the working electrode. A fall in ECSA between the start and end of a test confirms catalyst degradation, while an unchanged ECSA with falling performance points to water management or ionomer problems.
