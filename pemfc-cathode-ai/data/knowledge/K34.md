---
id: K34
title: Scaling relations between OOH and OH
topic: dft-descriptors
evidence: literature
tags: scaling relation, OOH, OH, volcano, overpotential floor
---
Across many metal and alloy surfaces the adsorption free energy of OOH tracks that of OH with a nearly constant offset of about 3.2 eV, with scatter of a few tenths of an eV. Because the two intermediates cannot be tuned independently, there is a minimum overpotential for surfaces that obey the relation: the two steps that involve them cannot both be optimised, and the best possible limiting potential on this picture lies several tenths of a volt below 1.23 V. Strategies that break the scaling, for example by stabilising OOH differently from OH, are discussed in the literature but are hard to realise in a working catalyst.
