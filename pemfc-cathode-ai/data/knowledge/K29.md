---
id: K29
title: Local oxygen transport resistance in the catalyst layer
topic: catalyst-layer
evidence: literature
tags: local transport, ionomer film, low loading, mass transport
---
Part of the high-current loss comes from oxygen moving through the thin ionomer film and water film around Pt particles. This resistance becomes more important as Pt loading falls, because the same current runs through less Pt surface. It does not depend on flow-field design, so raising flow rate does not remove it. Remedies include a more oxygen-permeable ionomer, a better ionomer distribution, a support with accessible pores for Pt, and a moderate rise in operating pressure.
