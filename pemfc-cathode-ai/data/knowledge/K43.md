title: Uncertainty-aware ML for DFT-guided screening
topic: dft-ml
evidence: textbook
tags: uncertainty, Gaussian process, applicability domain, active learning, leave-one-system-out
---
A model that predicts a catalyst descriptor from cheap composition features can rank candidates without new DFT, but only inside the chemistry it has seen. Gaussian processes and ensembles provide an uncertainty; evaluation should hold out whole chemical systems (all Pt-Co rows, then all Pt-Ni rows) to mimic a new alloying element, and the stated uncertainty should be checked for calibration. High-uncertainty or out-of-distribution candidates are the best targets for the next DFT calculation, because they add the most information.
