title: Reading a polarisation curve

A polarisation curve has three zones. At low current the voltage falls steeply from open-circuit voltage because of activation loss at the cathode. At mid current the curve is close to linear and its slope is an apparent area-specific resistance that contains ohmic resistance and part of the charge-transfer resistance. At high current the curve bends down at a knee because oxygen or water transport limits the reaction. Power density is current times voltage and peaks near the start of the knee. This project estimates each zone from the curve and compares it with curves measured at the same pressure and humidity.
