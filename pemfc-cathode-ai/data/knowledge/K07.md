title: Platinum dissolution, ripening and ECSA loss
topic: durability
evidence: textbook
tags: Pt dissolution, Ostwald ripening, ECSA, voltage decay
---
Pt particles lose surface area by dissolution and redeposition (Ostwald ripening), by migration and coalescence on the support, and by detachment. Potential cycling to high upper potentials, high temperature and high humidity speed this up. The result is lower electrochemically active surface area (ECSA), which lowers the kinetic part of the curve and causes voltage decay at constant current. ECSA from hydrogen underpotential deposition before and after a test is the direct check; a slow, irreversible decay with falling ECSA points to this mechanism rather than to temporary flooding.
