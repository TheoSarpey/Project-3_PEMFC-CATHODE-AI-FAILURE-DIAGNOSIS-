id: K12
title: Membrane thickness and GDL compression
topic: ohmic
evidence: literature
tags: Nafion 112, membrane, compression, contact resistance, GDL
---
Nafion 112 is a membrane about 50 micrometres thick. Thinner membranes cut ohmic resistance but allow more hydrogen crossover. Compression of the assembly sets the contact resistance between layers and the porosity of the gas diffusion layer. Too little compression raises contact resistance; too much crushes the GDL, closes pores and promotes flooding at high current. The ECSIM standard test compares 5 and 12 percent compression, so the trade-off can be read from the polarisation curves.
