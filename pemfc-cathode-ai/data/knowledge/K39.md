title: Coverage, solvation and field effects not captured by simple slab models
topic: dft-method
evidence: literature
tags: coverage, solvation, water, electric field, OH coverage
---
A single adsorbate on a vacuum slab omits the water network, the interfacial field and the effect of neighbouring adsorbates. Hydrogen bonding with water can stabilise OH and OOH by amounts of a few tenths of an electronvolt, and at realistic potentials the surface carries a coverage of OH or O that changes binding. These corrections are partly systematic across similar surfaces, which is why relative rankings can survive, but a ranking from vacuum calculations is a hypothesis to test, not a result.
