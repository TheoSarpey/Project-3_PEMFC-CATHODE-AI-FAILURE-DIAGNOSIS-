title: Flooding versus dehydration (water management)
topic: water
evidence: textbook
tags: flooding, dehydration, water management, HFR
---
A PEM fuel cell needs the membrane wet enough to conduct protons yet pores open enough to pass gas. Flooding fills cathode pores or the gas diffusion layer with liquid water and blocks oxygen: it appears as an early mass-transport knee at high current, sometimes with noisy or falling voltage, and often recovers after a purge, higher flow or higher temperature. Dehydration dries the membrane and ionomer: it shows as a rising high-frequency resistance and a steeper linear region, and improves with humidification. Both can occur in the same cell at different current densities.
