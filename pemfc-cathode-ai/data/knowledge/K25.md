The ECSIM PEM Fuel Cell Dataset (Hamidi, Haghighi, Askari, ChemRxiv 2020, doi 10.26434/chemrxiv.11902023, CC BY 4.0) holds polarisation and impedance data for a standard test of a Nafion 112 cell and for eight membrane electrode assembly activation tests. Electrodes are Pt/C 20 wt% on carbon paper with about 0.38-0.39 mg Pt per cm2. Pressure is 5, 15 or 25 psig and relative humidity 30, 50 or 100 percent after activation. The activation procedure uses constant voltage, cycling potential or constant current, in repeated sets.
