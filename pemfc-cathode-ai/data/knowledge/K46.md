title: Which DFT calculation to run next

For a promising candidate, first converge the setup: plane-wave cutoff, k-point mesh, slab thickness and vacuum, and relax with the bottom layers fixed. Calculate OH, O and OOH on several sites and keep the most stable, calculate Pt(111) with identical settings as a reference, and compare the functional on at least one benchmark. Then add the checks that change conclusions: coverage, water, segregation under adsorbate and a dissolution estimate. Kinetic barriers come last and only for the best candidates.
