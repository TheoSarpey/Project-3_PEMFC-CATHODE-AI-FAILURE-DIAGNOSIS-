---
id: K18
title: Oxygen versus air operation
topic: operating
evidence: textbook
tags: H2/O2, H2/air, oxygen partial pressure, mass transport
---
Pure oxygen has about five times the oxygen partial pressure of air at the same total pressure. In H2/O2 the concentration losses are much smaller and the kinetic part is stronger, so a cell can look healthier than it will under air. The ECSIM dataset reports H2/O2 operation, so a mass-transport limit measured there probably understates the limit in air. Conclusions about flooding and high-current performance should be re-checked in H2/air before they are used for a vehicle-type application.
