title: Activation (break-in) protocols
topic: protocol

A new membrane electrode assembly rarely gives its best performance at once. Conditioning hydrates the ionomer, improves contact between ionomer and Pt and clears surface contaminants, so power typically rises over the first cycles and then levels. Common routines use constant voltage, constant current or potential cycling, which are the three families in the ECSIM activation tests. A falling trend of power across activation sets is abnormal and should prompt checks of water balance, contamination and membrane integrity.
