id: K17
title: Contaminants and poisoning
topic: durability
evidence: literature
tags: CO, sulfur, cations, contamination, poisoning
---
Carbon monoxide at ppm levels poisons the anode Pt. Sulfur compounds adsorb strongly on cathode Pt and lower ORR activity. Metal cations from alloy leaching, corroded hardware or humidifier water exchange with protons in the ionomer and lower its conductivity. Siloxanes from seals can deposit on the electrode. Some poisoning is partly reversible by clean-gas operation or high-potential cycling, cation contamination often is not. A sudden activation loss with intact HFR points more to poisoning than to dehydration.
