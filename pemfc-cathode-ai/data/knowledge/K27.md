title: Scope and safety of this screening tool
topic: scope
evidence: textbook
tags: scope, safety, limitations, hydrogen
---
This product is a research screening aid. It ranks and explains curves; it does not replace electrochemical testing, hardware inspection or a safety review. Hydrogen is flammable and PEM test stands need the laboratory's own safety procedures. Do not change gas, pressure, temperature or voltage limits of a test stand on the basis of an automated suggestion without checking the stand's operating limits and the manufacturer's guidance.
