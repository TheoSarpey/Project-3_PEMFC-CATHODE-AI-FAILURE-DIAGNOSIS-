title: Surface segregation and ordering in alloys
topic: dft-stability
evidence: literature
tags: segregation energy, alloy ordering, Pt skin, subsurface
---
A segregation energy is the DFT energy change when a solute atom moves from the bulk of the alloy to its surface. A negative value means the solute prefers the surface and may be exposed to oxidation and dissolution; a positive value supports a Pt-rich skin. Adsorbates change segregation, because strongly binding oxygen species can pull the less noble metal outward. A DFT study of stability therefore needs the structure under adsorbate, not only the clean slab.
