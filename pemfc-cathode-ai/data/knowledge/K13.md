id: K13
title: Low open-circuit voltage: crossover, short, contamination
topic: diagnosis
evidence: textbook
tags: OCV, crossover, pinhole, internal short, contamination
---
In H2/O2 an open-circuit voltage close to 1 V is typical for a healthy Pt/C cell; a clearly lower value suggests hydrogen crossover through a thin or damaged membrane, an internal electrical short, a leak, or a poisoned or inactive cathode. Hydrogen crossover is checked with a linear sweep voltammetry crossover measurement; an internal short appears as a low shorting resistance. A low intercept of the linear region in the polarisation curve is a related, softer symptom and does not by itself separate these causes.
