id: K26
title: Limits of the ECSIM dataset
topic: dataset
evidence: dataset
tags: dataset limitations, single catalyst, H2/O2, generalisation
---
The data come from one catalyst type (Pt/C), a small laboratory cell, oxygen as oxidant and a limited design of pressure, humidity, ionomer content and compression. There are no repeat measurements across laboratories, no frequency axis in the impedance files, no ECSA or crossover data and no long-term durability test. It therefore cannot identify the best catalyst chemistry, cannot establish absolute failure thresholds, and cannot support claims about air operation. Catalyst-choice advice in this product comes from the literature notes and is labelled as such.
