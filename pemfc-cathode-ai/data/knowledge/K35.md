title: The ORR volcano and why Pt is close but not at the top
topic: dft-descriptors
evidence: literature
tags: volcano, Sabatier, Pt(111), binding energy, descriptor
---
Plotting the limiting potential against the OH adsorption free energy gives a volcano: surfaces that bind OH too strongly are limited by removing OH, and surfaces that bind it too weakly are limited by forming OOH. Calculations commonly place Pt(111) on the strong-binding side, slightly too strong by on the order of a tenth of an electronvolt. That is why alloying and strain, which weaken oxygen binding a little, are expected to help Pt. The result is a statement about thermodynamic trends in the model, not a measured current.
