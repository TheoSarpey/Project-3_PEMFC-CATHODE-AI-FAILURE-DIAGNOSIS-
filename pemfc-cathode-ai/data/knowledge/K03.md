id: K03
title: Platinum alloy cathode catalysts (PtCo, PtNi)
topic: catalyst
evidence: literature
tags: PtCo, PtNi, alloy, leaching, activity
---
Alloying Pt with transition metals such as Co or Ni weakens oxygen binding on the Pt surface, and half-cell studies commonly report several-fold higher specific activity than pure Pt. Gains measured in full membrane electrode assemblies are usually smaller than in rotating-disk tests. The main risk is leaching of the transition metal: the cations can exchange into the ionomer and membrane, lowering proton conductivity and adding oxygen-transport resistance. Alloys are therefore judged by activity after a durability protocol, not only fresh activity. The ECSIM dataset contains no alloy catalysts, so their benefit cannot be tested with this data.
