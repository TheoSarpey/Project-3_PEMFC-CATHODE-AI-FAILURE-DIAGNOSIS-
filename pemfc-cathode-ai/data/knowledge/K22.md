title: Metrics used to compare cathode catalysts
topic: metrics
evidence: literature
tags: mass activity, DOE target, power density at 0.6 V, ECSA
---
Common metrics are mass activity at 0.9 V corrected for resistance and hydrogen crossover, ECSA, power density at a fixed voltage such as 0.6 V, peak power, HFR and voltage decay after an accelerated durability test. US Department of Energy programme targets for cathode mass activity are often quoted near 0.44 A per mg of platinum-group metal at 0.9 V in H2/O2, but targets and test conditions are revised, so check the current programme documents before citing a number. This project uses power density, current at 0.6 V and the polarisation-zone losses because those are what the data supports.
