id: K21
title: Designing a reliable improvement experiment
topic: protocol
evidence: textbook
tags: design of experiments, replicate, iR correction, one variable
---
Change one variable at a time, keep the conditioning and measurement protocol identical, and repeat the experiment on more than one membrane electrode assembly because assembly-to-assembly spread can be as large as the effect studied. Report voltage with and without correction for the measured resistance, and state whether the gas is oxygen or air. Record pressure, humidity, temperature and flow with every curve. A difference smaller than the repeatability of the same cell on different days is not yet a result.
