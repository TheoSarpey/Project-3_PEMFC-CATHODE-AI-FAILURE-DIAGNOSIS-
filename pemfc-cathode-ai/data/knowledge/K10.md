title: Effect of operating pressure
topic: operating
evidence: textbook
tags: pressure, Nernst, oxygen partial pressure, backpressure
---
Raising cathode pressure raises oxygen partial pressure. The Nernst term gives a gain of about (RT/4F)*ln(p2/p1), which is only a few millivolts for a doubling, but the kinetic gain and the reduction of concentration losses are larger, so measured benefits are often tens of millivolts and a clear rise in peak power. In a real system the benefit must be weighed against compressor power. In the ECSIM data pressure is set to 5, 15 and 25 psig, which lets the pressure effect be measured directly at fixed humidity.
