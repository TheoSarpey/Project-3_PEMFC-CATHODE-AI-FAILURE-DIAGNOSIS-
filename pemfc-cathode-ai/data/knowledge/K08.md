---
id: K08
title: Ionomer content in the cathode catalyst layer
topic: catalyst-layer
evidence: literature
tags: Nafion, ionomer, Pt utilisation, proton conduction
---
Ionomer in the catalyst layer carries protons to the Pt and binds the layer. Too little ionomer leaves Pt particles without a proton path, especially at low humidity, so utilisation and kinetic performance drop. Too much ionomer thickens the film over the Pt and blocks pores, which raises oxygen transport resistance and shows up at high current. Reported optimum windows depend on the support, loading and humidity, so the optimum must be measured for each catalyst layer. The ECSIM standard test varies the Nafion content (20 and 25 percent) together with humidity, pressure and membrane compression.
