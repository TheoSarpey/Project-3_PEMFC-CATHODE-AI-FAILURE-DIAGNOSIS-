A table of catalyst descriptors can mix values from different origins: DFT calculations, machine-learned potentials, measurements, literature copies and model predictions. They are not interchangeable. Keep a provenance column, cite the source of each value, never present a predicted value as calculated, and do not use unreferenced or toy values to choose a catalyst. Predictions should carry an uncertainty and a note when the candidate lies outside the training chemistry.
