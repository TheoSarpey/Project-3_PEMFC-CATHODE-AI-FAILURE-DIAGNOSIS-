title: The d-band centre as a descriptor
topic: dft-descriptors
evidence: literature
tags: d-band centre, electronic structure, adsorption trend, DOS
---
The d-band centre is the average energy of the metal d states relative to the Fermi level. Within a family of similar transition-metal surfaces, a lower d-band centre often goes with weaker adsorption, so it can be used to screen alloys cheaply once a density of states is available. It is less reliable across very different chemistries, for magnetic elements and when sp states matter, and it still requires a DFT calculation of the electronic structure, so it is cheaper than a full adsorption study but not free.
