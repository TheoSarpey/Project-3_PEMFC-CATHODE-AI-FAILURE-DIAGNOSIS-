A good limiting potential is necessary, not sufficient. Fuel-cell performance also depends on the catalyst loading and particle size, how the ionomer covers the catalyst, water and oxygen transport in the layer, and durability under potential cycling. Rotating-disk activity usually overstates membrane-electrode-assembly activity. DFT screening narrows the candidates; membrane-electrode-assembly testing, as in the ECSIM data for Pt/C, is where the catalyst layer has to succeed.
