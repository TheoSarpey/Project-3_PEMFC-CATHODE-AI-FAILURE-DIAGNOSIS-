title: Machine-learned interatomic potentials for fast screening
topic: dft-ml
evidence: literature
tags: MLIP, MACE, CHGNet, foundation potential, screening, accuracy
---
Machine-learned interatomic potentials can relax structures and estimate adsorption energies orders of magnitude faster than DFT. Their errors are typically a few tenths of an electronvolt on adsorption energies and grow outside the chemistries they were trained on, which is the size of the effect screening tries to resolve. They are best used to generate candidate structures and a first ranking, with DFT re-calculation for the shortlist. Values from such a potential must carry a provenance tag that says so.
