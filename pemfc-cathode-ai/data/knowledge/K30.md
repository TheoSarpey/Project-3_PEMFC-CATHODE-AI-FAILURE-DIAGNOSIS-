Pore-size distribution and hydrophobicity decide where liquid water collects. A microporous layer with suitable PTFE content helps push product water away from the catalyst layer, while a layer that is too thick or too hydrophobic can dry the membrane. A thicker catalyst layer gives more Pt but longer transport paths. These structural choices change the mass-transport knee and the ohmic slope even when the catalyst itself is unchanged, so they must be controlled when two catalysts are compared.
