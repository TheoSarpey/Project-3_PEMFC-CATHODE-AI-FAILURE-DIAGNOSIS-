id: K28
title: Decay at constant current
topic: diagnosis
evidence: textbook
tags: voltage decay, constant current, reversible, irreversible
---
At constant current the voltage trend separates mechanisms by their time scale. Fast, recoverable drops after a gas or humidity change point to water management. A slow, monotonic loss over many hours that does not recover after a purge or a short high-humidity rest points to irreversible loss such as ECSA loss. The activation files with constant-current steps record the state afterwards, so changes across activation sets act as a coarse view of this trend.
