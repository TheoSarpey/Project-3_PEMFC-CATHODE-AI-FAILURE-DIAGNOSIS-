id: K01
title: ORR kinetics at the PEMFC cathode
topic: kinetics
evidence: textbook
tags: ORR, Tafel, activation loss, cathode
---
The oxygen reduction reaction (ORR) at the cathode is far slower than hydrogen oxidation at the anode, so the cathode causes most of the activation (kinetic) voltage loss in a PEM fuel cell. On Pt the reaction proceeds mainly by the four-electron path to water. Textbook Tafel slopes for Pt are roughly 60-70 mV per decade at low current, but apparent slopes in a full cell vary with humidity, pressure and catalyst-layer structure. Raising oxygen partial pressure raises the effective exchange current, which lifts the low-current part of the polarisation curve. In a polarisation curve the kinetic zone is the steep fall from open-circuit voltage at low current density.
