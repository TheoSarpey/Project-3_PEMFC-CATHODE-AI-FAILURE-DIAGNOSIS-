title: Symptom-to-cause guide for cathode failures
topic: diagnosis
evidence: textbook
tags: troubleshooting, symptoms, checklist, failure modes
---
Low open-circuit voltage: check crossover, internal short, leaks and contamination. High and rising HFR: check humidification, membrane hydration and compression. Early high-current knee with unstable voltage: check flooding, flow rate, temperature and backpressure. Slow irreversible voltage decay: check ECSA loss, carbon corrosion and cation contamination. Low activity from the start: check catalyst loading, ink quality, ionomer content and activation. A polarisation curve narrows the list; confirmation needs a second measurement such as EIS, CV, crossover LSV or a recovery procedure.
