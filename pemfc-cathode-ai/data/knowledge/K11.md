id: K11
title: Temperature and humidity interplay
topic: operating
evidence: textbook
tags: temperature, relative humidity, conductivity
---
Higher cell temperature speeds ORR kinetics and mass diffusion, but it lowers the relative humidity of the gas at fixed water content and can dry the membrane. Membrane proton conductivity rises steeply with water content, so low relative humidity usually raises ohmic resistance. The practical optimum is the temperature and humidity at which the membrane stays hydrated without flooding the cathode at the target current. The ECSIM activation sets use 30, 50 and 100 percent relative humidity at fixed cell temperature, so humidity effects can be separated from temperature effects only within that design.
