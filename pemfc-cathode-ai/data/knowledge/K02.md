title: Pt/C as the baseline cathode catalyst
topic: catalyst

Platinum nanoparticles of a few nanometres on high-area carbon (Pt/C) are the reference cathode catalyst. Two numbers describe activity: specific activity (per unit Pt surface) and mass activity (per mass of Pt). Loading is a trade-off: more Pt gives a thicker catalyst layer and more kinetic current, but also longer oxygen and proton paths. At very low Pt loading the oxygen resistance local to the Pt surface becomes noticeable, so reducing loading is not free. In the ECSIM dataset both electrodes are 20 wt% Pt/C with a loading of about 0.38-0.39 mg Pt per cm2.
