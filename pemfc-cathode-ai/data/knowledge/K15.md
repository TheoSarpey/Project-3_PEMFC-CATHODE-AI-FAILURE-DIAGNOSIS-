In a Nyquist plot the intercept of the arc with the real axis at high frequency is the high-frequency resistance (HFR), which is dominated by membrane and contact resistance. The diameter of the arc is the sum of charge-transfer and transport resistances and shrinks as current rises. A rising HFR points to drying or contact problems; a growing arc points to kinetic or transport trouble. The ECSIM impedance files give real and imaginary parts but no frequency column, so this project only uses the smallest real part as an HFR proxy and the real-axis span of the sampled arc as an arc-width proxy.
