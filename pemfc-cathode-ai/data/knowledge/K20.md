title: Operating strategies that protect the cathode
topic: durability
evidence: literature
tags: mitigation, upper potential, load cycling, humidity control
---
General practice to slow cathode degradation includes limiting time at high potential, avoiding repeated cycling across the Pt oxidation range, controlling humidity and temperature, using clean gases and preventing starvation. Which of these matters most depends on the catalyst: alloys fear cation release, Fe-N-C fears peroxide, Pt/C fears dissolution and carbon corrosion. Choose mitigation from the diagnosed mechanism rather than applying a fixed list.
