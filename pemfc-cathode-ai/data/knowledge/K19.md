title: Start-stop, fuel starvation and cell reversal
topic: durability
evidence: textbook
tags: start-stop, starvation, reversal, carbon corrosion
---
Local fuel starvation can drive the anode potential high enough to oxidise carbon or water, and in severe cases the cell voltage reverses. Start-up and shut-down with air in the anode channel creates high local cathode potentials. Mitigations are purge and start-up strategies, reversal-tolerant anode catalysts that favour water oxidation, and monitoring of single-cell voltages in a stack. These mechanisms are not testable with the ECSIM single-cell data.
