id: K04
title: Structured and shape-controlled Pt catalysts
topic: catalyst
evidence: literature
tags: core-shell, nanoframe, intermetallic, Pt3Ni
---
Extended Pt3Ni(111) surfaces, nanoframes, core-shell particles and ordered intermetallic PtCo or PtFe particles have shown very high ORR activity in model studies. The recurring difficulty is translation to a working electrode: particle stability under potential cycling, scale-up, and the interaction with ionomer in a thick catalyst layer. Ordered intermetallics are attractive because their structure resists dissolution better than disordered alloys. Treat any claimed activity as conditional on the test protocol (temperature, potential window, H2/O2 versus H2/air) and check that it was confirmed in a membrane electrode assembly.
