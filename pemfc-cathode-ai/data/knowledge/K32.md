title: The four-step ORR mechanism and the limiting potential
topic: dft-method
evidence: textbook
tags: ORR, associative mechanism, limiting potential, overpotential
---
In the associative four-electron mechanism, O2 is protonated and reduced to OOH on the surface, then OOH becomes adsorbed O plus water, O becomes OH, and OH is released as water. With the computational hydrogen electrode each step has a free energy at 0 V; the four add up to -4.92 eV for 2 H2O formation. The theoretical limiting potential is the highest potential at which every step is still downhill, and the overpotential is 1.23 V minus that value. The step with the largest free energy is the limiting step, and which step limits tells you whether the surface binds oxygen intermediates too strongly or too weakly.
