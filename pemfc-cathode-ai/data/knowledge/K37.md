title: Strain and ligand effects in Pt alloys and Pt skins
topic: dft-descriptors
evidence: literature
tags: strain, ligand effect, Pt skin, alloy, core-shell
---
Alloying changes adsorption on Pt in two ways: the lattice contracts or expands (strain) and the neighbouring atoms change the electronic structure (ligand effect). A compressed Pt surface generally binds oxygen intermediates more weakly. In acid the surface of a Pt-rich alloy is often a Pt-enriched skin over a non-noble subsurface; whether that skin forms and survives depends on the composition, the potential and the preparation. A model of a skin is a choice of structure, not a prediction of what the real catalyst looks like.
